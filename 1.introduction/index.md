# Introduction to Apie

Apie is a Domain-Driven Design (DDD) library that allows you to focus on your business logic while it handles the "glue code" for APIs, CMS, and more.

In this section, you will find:

1. [What is Apie?](./1.what-is-apie.md) - An overview of the philosophy and goals of Apie.
2. [Key features](./2.key-features.md) - Why you should use Apie.
3. [Use cases](./3.use-cases.md) - Common scenarios where Apie shines.
4. [Roadmap](./4.roadmap.md) - The future of Apie.

## Domain Objects

Apie is centered around domain objects. In this section, you will find more details about the different types of domain objects:

5. [Domain Objects](./5.domain-objects.md) - An overview of the different types of domain objects.
6. [Value Objects](./6.value-objects.md) - Small, immutable objects.
7. [Composite Value Objects](./7.composite-value-objects.md) - Value objects consisting of multiple properties.
8. [Entities](./8.entities.md) - Mutable objects with a unique identifier.
9. [Polymorphic Entities](./9.polymorphic-entities.md) - Entities with inheritance.
10. [Root Aggregates](./10.root-aggregates.md) - Entities that manage child entities.
11. [Enums](./11.enums.md) - Standard PHP enums.
12. [Lists, Sets and Hashmaps](./12.lists-sets-hashmaps.md) - Specialized collection classes.
13. [DTOs](./13.dtos.md) - Simple data transfer objects.
